# Overview
This document highlights the quality profiles of the medias downloaded through Sonarr or Radarr

> [!TIP] Note
> There values aren’t "The Best", they are just what I prefer, If you want to have larger or smaller file sized depending on the storage you have or the quality you prefer, feel free to tweak and change the values
